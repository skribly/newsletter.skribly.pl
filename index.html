How the weekly issue is made

Short, checked and with its sources in plain sight. Four steps, every week. The daily digest skips the check by a person and says so.

  1. 1

    Gathered from primary sources

    We read the labs' own posts, release notes, papers and repositories, not other newsletters. Every item links to where it came from.

  2. 2

    Drafted with an agent

    The newsletter skill of agent-skills gathers the news of the week and writes a first draft in English and Polish.

  3. 3

    Checked by a person

    A person reads every item against its source, cuts what does not hold and approves the issue. Nothing is sent without that.

  4. 4

    Sent every week

    One e-mail in your language, on your topics, beside the daily digest. Every issue is also in the archive here and in the RSS feed.
